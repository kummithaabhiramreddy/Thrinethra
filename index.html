Platform architecture

Process at the edge. Decide at the center.

Onboard hardware does the heavy vision work on the bus itself, so only compact, meaningful events leave the vehicle — not raw video. That keeps bandwidth low and the central platform fast.

01 · ONBOARD, PER BUS
Edge processing unit
  • Ingests front, rear, side and cabin camera streams
  • Runs detection, classification and tracking models locally
  • Tags every event with timestamp and GPS
  • Buffers and syncs over available network, no continuous upload
02 · IN TRANSIT
Event, not footage
  • Compact structured alert: type, location, confidence, clip
  • Encrypted transmission to command platform
  • Priority queue for safety-critical events
  • Full clips retrievable on demand, not streamed by default
03 · CENTRAL PLATFORM
Fleet-wide command center
  • Aggregates events across the entire fleet in real time
  • Plots everything on a GIS map with congestion heat layers
  • Builds origin–destination and delay analytics
  • Routes incidents to the right authority automatically

Incident response

From dashcam frame to actionable alert

A rash-driving or hit-and-run event moves through the same pipeline in seconds, without anyone reviewing footage by hand.

STEP 01
Detect
Onboard model flags abnormal driving behaviour or a collision event in the live stream.
STEP 02
Track
The offending vehicle is tracked across frames to hold a stable view of its plate.
STEP 03
Read plate
Registration number is extracted with a confidence score, not a bare guess.
STEP 04
Tag
Timestamp and GPS location are attached automatically at the point of detection.
STEP 05
Alert
A secure alert reaches the central command system for the authority to act on.

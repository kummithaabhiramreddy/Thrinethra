Terms of Service

Effective September 2026

These terms govern access to the Thrinethra platform by transport authorities and their authorized staff. By creating an account you agree to use fleet event data, dashboards and reports only for lawful transport planning, safety and maintenance purposes.

  • Accounts are issued to verified transport authority organisations, not individual members of the public.
  • Detected events (road hazards, congestion, incidents) are generated by automated models and may contain errors; they support decision-making but are not a substitute for on-site verification.
  • Access may be suspended for misuse, including sharing account credentials outside the authorised organisation.

Data Handling Policy

Thrinethra processes video on board each bus using edge AI. Raw video is not transmitted to the central platform by default — only structured events (type, location, timestamp, confidence score, and a short clip where relevant) leave the vehicle.

What we store

  • Detected event metadata: category, GPS location, timestamp, confidence score
  • Vehicle registration numbers extracted during incident detection, retained per your authority's retention policy
  • Aggregated traffic and congestion statistics

What stays on the vehicle

  • Raw camera footage, unless specifically requested for incident review
  • Cabin camera footage is processed for safety detection only and is not exported by default

Retention & access

Retention periods and internal access controls are configured per transport authority in line with applicable local regulations. Your organisation's administrator controls who on your team can view incident-level detail versus aggregated analytics.
